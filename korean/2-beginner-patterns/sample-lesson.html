Beginner Pattern Lesson

Say what you like

Use 을/를 with 좋아해요 to talk about likes and dislikes.

Today's patterns ~을/를 좋아해요

Particle Note

Choose by the final sound.

받침 ends with a consonant → 을 밥을 물을 책을 음악을
받침 없음 ends with a vowel → 를 커피를 영화를 티비를
