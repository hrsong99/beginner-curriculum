Part 1

~을/를 좋아해요

I like... / I don't like...

Learn

Pattern 1

Read each pattern and its example with me.

~을/를 좋아해요. I like...
커피를 좋아해요. I like coffee.
~을/를 안 좋아해요. I don't like... — 안 goes right before 좋아해요.
커피를 안 좋아해요. I don't like coffee.
Learn

을 or 를?

Look at the last sound of the word.

받침 ends with a consonant → 을 밥을 책을 음악을
받침 없음 ends with a vowel → 를 커피를 영화를 티비를

빵을 안 좋아해요.I don't like bread.
티비를 안 좋아해요.I don't like TV.

Part 2

~을/를 좋아해요?

Do you like...? / What do you like?

Learn

Pattern 2

A question is the same sentence with a rising tone.

~을/를 좋아해요? Do you like...?
빵을 좋아해요? Do you like bread?
뭘 좋아해요? What do you like?
뭘 좋아해요? — 커피를 좋아해요. What do you like? — I like coffee.
좋아해요. statement ↘ voice falls
좋아해요? question ↗ voice rises

Roleplay

You just met a new friend at a cafe — complete all four missions in our conversation.

  • Say hello. 안녕하세요!
  • Ask me what I like. 뭘 좋아해요?
  • Tell me one thing you like and one thing you don't. ~을/를 (안) 좋아해요.
