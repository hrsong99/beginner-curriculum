Beginner Pattern Lesson

Say what you like

Use 을/를 with 좋아해요 to talk about likes and dislikes.

Today's patterns [noun]을/를 좋아해요
[noun]을/를 안 좋아해요 I like... / I do not like...

Tutor Greeting

Listen and understand.

안녕하세요! 또 만났네요. Hello. Good to see you again.
오늘도 재미있게 공부해 봐요. Let's have a fun lesson today.

Review Endings

Complete each sentence with 예요 or 이에요.

이거는 책____. This is a book.
저는 학생____. I am a student.

Translate One Sentence

Translate one sentence into Korean.

That is coffee. that=저거 coffee=커피

Today's Patterns

Read the two patterns for today.

[noun]을/를 좋아해요. I like [noun].
커피를 좋아해요.I like coffee.
[noun]을/를 안 좋아해요. I do not like [noun].
커피를 안 좋아해요.I do not like coffee.

Pick 을 or 를

Look at the last sound of the noun.

을 after a consonant sound 밥을 물을 책을 음악을
를 after a vowel sound 커피를 영화를 티비를

Write Likes

Write positive sentences with 좋아해요.

I like books. book=책 like=~을/를 좋아해요
I like water. water=물 like=~을/를 좋아해요

Write a Dislike

Write one negative sentence with 안 좋아해요.

I do not like coffee. coffee=커피 not like=안 좋아해요

Roleplay

Complete all three missions.

  • Ask me what I like. 뭘 좋아해요?
  • Tell me one thing you do not like. 안 좋아해요.
  • Ask me if I like coffee. 커피를 좋아해요?
