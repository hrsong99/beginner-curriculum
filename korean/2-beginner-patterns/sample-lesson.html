Beginner Pattern Lesson

을/를 좋아해요

Say what you like, what you do not like, and ask someone back.

Today's pattern What do you like?

Warm Up

소리 내어 읽어 보세요.

안녕하세요! 또 만났네요.Hello. Good to see you again.
오늘도 재미있게 공부해 봐요.Let's have a fun lesson today.

Review

Complete each sentence with 예요 or 이에요.

이거는 책____. This is a book.
저는 학생____. I am a student.
Translate: That is coffee. Hint: 저거 = that, 커피 = coffee

Today's Patterns

Notice the particle, then keep 좋아해요 fixed.

Concept 1 을 / 를 object particle
Consonant ending: 밥을, 물을, 책을, 음악을
Vowel ending: 커피를, 영화를, 티비를
Concept 2 좋아해요 like
커피를 좋아해요.I like coffee.
커피를 안 좋아해요.I do not like coffee.
커피를 좋아해요?Do you like coffee?
뭘 좋아해요?What do you like?
Practice 1

Statements

First read the pattern. Then write one complete sentence at a time.

Drill A1

Read each sentence aloud.

Read
밥을 좋아해요.I like rice.
음악을 좋아해요.I like music.
빵을 안 좋아해요.I do not like bread.
티비를 안 좋아해요.I do not like TV.

Drill A2

Write positive sentences with 좋아해요.

Write I like books. Hint: book = 책 · pattern = noun + 을/를 좋아해요
I like water. Hint: water = 물

Drill A3

Write one negative sentence with 안 좋아해요.

I do not like coffee. Hint: coffee = 커피 · negative = 안 좋아해요

Short Dialogues

Say the full line.

Tutor
뭘 좋아해요?What do you like?
You
Hint: I like music. / music = 음악
You
Hint: Do you like bread? / bread = 빵
Tutor
네, 빵을 좋아해요.

Conversation

Read the dialogue with roles.

Yuna
알렉스 씨, 뭘 좋아해요?Alex, what do you like?
Alex
저는 영화를 좋아해요. 유나 씨는 뭘 좋아해요?I like movies. What do you like, Yuna?
Yuna
저는 음악을 좋아해요. 책도 좋아해요?I like music. Do you like books too?
Alex
아니요, 책은 안 좋아해요.No, I do not like books.

Free Talk

Answer truthfully, then ask back.

뭘 좋아해요?
뭘 안 좋아해요?
커피를 좋아해요?

Roleplay

Complete all three missions.

  • Ask me what I like. 뭘 좋아해요?
  • Tell me one thing you do not like. 안 좋아해요.
  • Ask me if I like coffee. 커피를 좋아해요?

Travel Use

Use the pattern to choose food.

Friend
배고파요! 불고기 좋아해요?I'm hungry. Do you like bulgogi?
You
Hint: No, I do not like meat. / meat = 고기
Friend
그럼 뭘 좋아해요?Then what do you like?
You
Hint: I like gimbap. / gimbap = 김밥
