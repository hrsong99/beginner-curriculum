Intermediate Expression Lesson

약속 잡기

Ask when someone is free, suggest a time, and close the plan.

Today's situation Making plans

Goal

오늘은 문법이 아니에요. 약속 잡기를 배워요.

Ask when someone is free.
Suggest a specific time.
Say yes, or softly say that day is tough.
Close the plan naturally.

Expressions

Read each phrase as a whole chunk.

Ask
언제 괜찮으세요?When works for you?
혹시 내일 괜찮으세요?Is tomorrow okay, by any chance?
혹시 주말 괜찮으세요?Is the weekend okay?
혹시 3시 괜찮으세요?Is 3 o'clock okay?
Replies
네, 좋아요.Sounds good.
죄송한데, 그날은 좀...Sorry, that day is a little...
그럼 그때 봬요.Then, see you then.
TIP

혹시 means "by any chance." Put it before an ask to make it softer.

Roleplay

Make a plan with me.

  • Ask when I am free. 언제 괜찮으세요?
  • Suggest a specific time. 혹시 + time + 괜찮으세요?
  • Lock it in and close. 그럼 그때 봬요.
