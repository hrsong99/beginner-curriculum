# Korean Course Catalog

A catalog of courses Korean learners actually want, all teachable in our chunk-based way: **one idea per page, 2–3 patterns or expressions per lesson**, practiced receptive → productive. Beginner courses teach **grammar patterns**; intermediate courses teach **expressions native speakers really use**.

**9 courses · 48 lessons total.**

---

## Foundation

### 한글 읽기 — Read Hangul · 5 lessons
*Decode and read Korean before any speaking course (teach → drill, no grammar).*

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… | Teaches |
|---|--------|--------------------|---------|
| 1 | 기본 모음 | Read the basic vowels | ㅏ ㅓ ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ ㅣ |
| 2 | 기본 자음 | Read the basic consonants | ㄱ ㄴ ㄷ ㄹ ㅁ ㅂ ㅅ … |
| 3 | 글자 조합 | Blend consonant + vowel into a syllable | 가 / 나 / 노 / 미 |
| 4 | 받침 | Read syllables with a final consonant | 밥, 책, 물, 강 |
| 5 | 읽기 연습 | Read real words out loud | 안녕, 사랑, 한국 |

## Beginner — Pattern-Based

### 기초 문장 — Your First Sentences · 6 lessons
*Build simple statements and questions from scratch.*

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… | Patterns |
|---|--------|--------------------|----------|
| 1 | 이에요/예요 | Say what something is | 이에요 / 예요 |
| 2 | 은/는 · 이/가 | Talk about yourself and topics | 저는 ~ / ~이·가 |
| 3 | 을/를 좋아해요 | Say what you like / don't like | 을·를 좋아해요 / 안 좋아해요 |
| 4 | 있어요/없어요 | Say what you have or what exists | 있어요 / 없어요 / ~에 있어요 |
| 5 | ~해요 (현재) | Talk about daily actions | 가요 / 먹어요 / 봐요 |
| 6 | 뭐·어디·언제 | Ask simple wh-questions | 뭐예요? / 어디예요? / 언제예요? |

## Intermediate — Expression-Based

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… | Expressions |

*Ask for things and make plans without sounding stiff.*

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… | Expressions |
|---|--------|--------------------|-------------|
| 1 | 혹시…? | Soften any question | 혹시 ~? / 혹시 ~ 있어요? |

| 5 | 마무리 | Lock in and close a plan | 그럼 그때 봬요 / 그렇게 해요 |
