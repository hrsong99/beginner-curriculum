# Korean Curriculum Table of Contents

Simple planning order for the three Korean lesson tracks. Beginner Patterns and Intermediate Expressions generally keep to two teachable concepts per lesson.

## 1. Hangul Teaching

| Lesson | Title | Concepts / Skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 한글 첫걸음: First Korean Words | Block idea: consonant + vowel = one syllable; first regular set: ㅇ, ㄴ, ㅁ + ㅏ ㅓ ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ ㅣ |
| 2 | More Regular Blocks | Add ㄱ, ㄷ, ㄹ, ㅂ; keep reading open syllables only |
| 3 | Soft Sounds | Add ㅅ, ㅈ, ㅎ; introduce common reading examples without sound-change overload |
| 4 | Strong Sounds | Add ㅋ, ㅌ, ㅍ, ㅊ; compare plain vs. aspirated sounds |
| 5 | Double Consonants | Add ㄲ, ㄸ, ㅃ, ㅆ, ㅉ; practice visual recognition and controlled reading |
| 6 | More Vowels | Add ㅑ, ㅕ, ㅛ, ㅠ; contrast basic vs. y-vowels |
| 7 | Combination Vowels | Add ㅐ, ㅔ, ㅚ, ㅟ, ㅢ, ㅘ, ㅝ, ㅙ, ㅞ; read common words |
| 8 | Batchim: Bottom Slot | Final consonant concept; one block can have a bottom consonant |
| 9 | Final-Sound Groups | Seven final sound groups: ㄱ, ㄴ, ㄷ, ㄹ, ㅁ, ㅂ, ㅇ |
| 10 | Linking | Batchim before ㅇ links into the next syllable |
| 11 | Essential Sound Changes | Only the highest-frequency changes for beginner reading |
| 12 | Hangul Fluency Check | Read short signs, names, menus, and simple learner sentences |

## 2. Beginner Patterns

| Lesson | Pattern Goal | Concept 1 | Concept 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I am / this is | `저는 [name]이에요/예요` | `이거/저거는 [noun]이에요/예요` |
| 2 | Basic descriptions | adjective + `-아요/어요` as fixed chunks | yes/no questions by intonation |
| 3 | Have / do not have | `[noun]이/가 있어요` | `[noun]이/가 없어요` |
| 4 | Where things are | `[place]에 있어요` | `어디에 있어요?` |
| 5 | Like / dislike | object particle `을/를` | `[noun]을/를 좋아해요 / 안 좋아해요` |
| 6 | Eat / drink | `[food]을/를 먹어요` | `[drink]을/를 마셔요` |
| 7 | Go / come | `[place]에 가요` | `[place]에 와요` |
| 8 | Do activities | `[noun]을/를 해요` | `뭐 해요?` |
| 9 | Want | `[noun]을/를 원해요` or `[verb]고 싶어요` | asking `뭐 하고 싶어요?` |
| 10 | Can / cannot | `[verb]을/ㄹ 수 있어요` | `[verb]을/ㄹ 수 없어요` |
| 11 | Past basics | `-았어요/었어요` as high-frequency fixed forms | yesterday / last week time words |
| 12 | Future basics | `-을/ㄹ 거예요` | tomorrow / later / weekend plans |
| 13 | Reasons | `왜요?` | `왜냐하면...` with simple reason chunks |
| 14 | Beginner Review Mission | combine identity, location, likes, actions, time, and plans | short guided conversation |

## 3. Intermediate Expressions

| Lesson | Situation | Concept 1 | Concept 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Making plans | `언제 괜찮으세요?` / `혹시 [time] 괜찮으세요?` | accept, decline, and close: `네, 좋아요`, `죄송한데...`, `그럼 그때 봬요` |
| 2 | Rescheduling | `혹시 다른 날도 괜찮으세요?` | propose alternatives: `그럼 [day/time] 어때요?` |
| 3 | Canceling politely | `죄송한데 못 갈 것 같아요` | add a light reason without overexplaining |
| 4 | Ordering at a cafe | `[item] 하나 주세요` | options: `따뜻한 거 / 차가운 거` |
| 5 | Restaurant basics | `[item] 주세요` / `추천해 주세요` | checking: `이거 매워요?` |
| 6 | Shopping | `이거 얼마예요?` | `입어 봐도 돼요? / 써 봐도 돼요?` |
| 7 | Directions | `[place] 어디예요?` | `왼쪽/오른쪽/직진` direction chunks |
| 8 | Transportation | `[place]까지 가요?` | `얼마나 걸려요?` |
| 9 | Asking for help | `도와주실 수 있어요?` | `잘 모르겠어요 / 다시 말씀해 주세요` |
| 10 | Checking understanding | `무슨 뜻이에요?` | `천천히 말해 주세요` |
| 11 | Opinions | `저는 [noun]이/가 좋아요` | soft disagreement: `저는 조금 달라요` |
| 12 | Invitations | `같이 [activity]할래요?` | accepting or declining naturally |
| 13 | Small talk | weather/day/weekend openers | follow-up questions |
| 14 | Intermediate Review Mission | make plans, order, ask for help, and handle changes | natural short roleplays |
