# Korean Curriculum — Table of Contents

**Goal:** take a Japanese learner from *never having seen Korean* to **reading any syllable aloud and using the basic grammar patterns of Korean**.

**32 lessons in two stages.** One clear outcome per lesson. (Beginner-pattern order follows `0-dekiru-kankokugo/dekiru-kankokugo-1-pattern-map.md`.)

---

## Stage 1 · 한글 Reading (12 lessons)

Decode every Korean syllable, one small load at a time.

| # | Lesson | Core outcome — you can read… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First Korean words | ㅇ ㄴ ㅁ + ㅏ ㅓ ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ ㅣ → 나 · 너 · 나무 |
| 2 | More regular blocks | + ㄱ ㄷ ㄹ ㅂ → 고기 · 다리 · 바다 |
| 3 | Soft sounds | + ㅅ ㅈ ㅎ → 사자 · 하마 |
| 4 | Strong sounds | + ㅋ ㅌ ㅍ ㅊ → 코 · 포도 · 커피 |
| 5 | Double consonants | + ㄲ ㄸ ㅃ ㅆ ㅉ → 토끼 · 아빠 |
| 6 | More vowels | + ㅑ ㅕ ㅛ ㅠ → 우유 · 여자 |
| 7 | Combination vowels | + ㅐ ㅔ ㅘ ㅝ ㅚ ㅟ ㅢ → 사과 · 의자 |
| 8 | Batchim (bottom slot) | a block with a final consonant → 산 · 책 |
| 9 | Final-sound groups | the 7 final sounds → 밥 · 한국 · 사람 |
| 10 | Linking | batchim + ㅇ links over → 한국어 · 음악 |
| 11 | Essential sound changes | the few high-frequency changes → 좋아요 |
| 12 | Fluency check | short signs, names, menus, simple sentences |

**After Stage 1:** any syllable can be read aloud.

## Stage 2 · Beginner Patterns (20 lessons)

One core pattern per lesson — from recognizing it to producing it.

| # | Lesson | Core outcome — you can… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Identity & topic | introduce yourself — `저는 [N]입니다` · `은/는` |
| 2 | Not identity | say what something isn't — `이/가 아닙니다` · `이/가` |
| 3 | This / that / what | say & ask what something is — `이에요/예요` |
| 4 | Have / don't have | say what you have or don't — `있어요 / 없어요` |
| 5 | Where things are | say where something is — `[place]에 있어요` + position words |
| 6 | Basic actions | make & link polite sentences — `-ㅂ/습니다` · `-고` |
| 7 | Negation & ranges | say you don't do something & give ranges — `안 / -지 않다` · `부터/까지` |
| 8 | Dates & prices | say dates & prices — Sino-Korean numbers · `-네요` |
| 9 | Everyday polite verbs | talk casually about actions — `-아/어요` |
| 10 | Time & counters | tell time & count things — native numbers + counters |
| 11 | Past basics | talk about the past — `-았/었어요` |
| 12 | Honorific basics | speak respectfully about others — `-(으)세요` |
| 13 | Honorific past | ask about someone's past respectfully — `-(으)셨어요` |
| 14 | Confirmation & contrast | confirm & contrast — `-지요?` · `-지만` |
| 15 | Want | say what you want to do — `-고 싶어요` |
| 16 | Reasons | give reasons — `-아/어서` |
| 17 | Permission & conditions | ask permission & state conditions — `-아/어도 돼요?` · `-(으)면` |
| 18 | Suggestions & purpose | suggest & state purpose — `-(으)ㄹ까요?` · `-(으)러` |
| 19 | Requests | make polite requests — `-아/어 주세요 / 주시겠어요?` |
| 20 | Can / cannot / doing now | say what you can do & are doing — `-(으)ㄹ 수 있어요` · `-고 있어요` |

**After Stage 2:** the learner has the full basic-grammar foundation — ready for the Intermediate Expressions track (situational conversation).
