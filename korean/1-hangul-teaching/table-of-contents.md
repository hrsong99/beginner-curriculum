# Hangul Reading Curriculum — Table of Contents

**Goal:** take a Japanese learner from *never having seen Korean* to **reading any Korean syllable aloud**.

**9 lessons.** Each lesson adds one small load — a few new letters, no cramming, no exceptions. Letters stay on simple open syllables (consonant + vowel) until the bottom slot (받침) is introduced in Part 3.

---
