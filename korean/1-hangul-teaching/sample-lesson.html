One Block

One square = one sound.

ㄴ + ㅏ → 나 consonant + vowel = one syllable

Listen as I say it, then say it with me.

나

The 6 Vowels

The silent ㅇ is just an empty seat.

아ㅏ as in father
어ㅓ as in son
오ㅗ as in go
우ㅜ as in moon
으ㅡ flat lips
이ㅣ as in see

Read aloud:

아 어 오 우 으 이
