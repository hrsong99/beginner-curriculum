# Japanese Beginner Speaking Curriculum (Pattern-First)

> A ready-to-teach table of contents for beginner learners who want to start talking right away.
>
> **Design rules**
> - Speak from Lesson 1.
> - Teach sentence patterns before abstract grammar labels.
> - Add one main new grammar move at a time, then immediately recycle it.
> - Use slot drills, substitution drills, Q&A drills, and short chained responses in every lesson.
> - Teach kana and pronunciation in parallel, not as a gate before speaking.

## Phase 1: Start Talking Immediately (Lessons 1-8)

Lesson 1:  
I can greet people, introduce myself, and ask who someone is  
1. 私は 〜です (watashi wa 〜 desu) (I am [Name / Job / Nationality])  
2. 〜さんですか (〜san desu ka) (Are you [Name]?)  
Support chunks: はじめまして / はい、そうです / いいえ、ちがいます  
Drill slots: names, countries, jobs, languages

Lesson 2:  
I can identify things and say who they belong to  
1. これは 〜です (kore wa 〜 desu) (This is [Object])  
2. これは 〜の 〜です (kore wa 〜 no 〜 desu) (This is [Someone]'s [Object])  
Support chunks: それは 〜です / あれは 〜です / なんですか  
Drill slots: phone, bag, book, pen, key, teacher, friend

Lesson 3:  
I can ask where places and things are  
1. 〜は どこですか (〜 wa doko desu ka) (Where is [Place / Object]?)  
2. 〜は ここ / そこ / あそこです (〜 wa koko / soko / asoko desu) ([Place / Object] is here / there / over there)  
Support chunks: こちら / そちら / あちら / トイレは どこですか  
Drill slots: restroom, station, classroom, exit, bank, café

Lesson 4:  
I can say what and who is in a place  
1. 〜に [Object] が あります (〜 ni [Object] ga arimasu) (There is [Object] in / at [Place])  
2. 〜に [Person / Animal] が います (〜 ni [Person / Animal] ga imasu) (There is [Person / Animal] in / at [Place])  
Support chunks: うえ / した / まえ / うしろ / なか  
Drill slots: room, desk, chair, cat, teacher, friend, park

Lesson 5:  
I can shop, ask prices, and request things  
1. 〜は いくらですか (〜 wa ikura desu ka) (How much is [Item]?)  
2. 〜を ください (〜 o kudasai) (Please give me [Item])  
Support chunks: これを ください / ありますか / もう ひとつ ください  
Drill slots: drinks, snacks, tickets, notebooks, umbrellas, bread

Lesson 6:  
I can tell time and talk about simple schedules  
1. 今、〜時 〜分です (ima, 〜ji 〜fun desu) (Right now, it is [Hour] [Minute])  
2. 〜時に 〜ます (〜ji ni 〜masu) (I [Verb] at [Time])  
Support chunks: 〜から 〜まで / なんじですか / げつようび / きょう  
Drill slots: wake up, eat, study, work, sleep, start time, end time

Lesson 7:  
I can talk about my daily actions and what I do not do  
1. 〜を [Verb]ます (〜 o [Verb]masu) (I [Verb] [Object])  
2. 〜を [Verb]ません (〜 o [Verb]masen) (I do not [Verb] [Object])  
Support chunks: いつも / よく / ときどき / あまり 〜ません  
Drill slots: eat breakfast, drink coffee, watch TV, read books, study Japanese

Lesson 8:  
I can say where I go and how I get there  
1. 〜に 行きます (〜 ni ikimasu) (I go to [Place])  
2. 〜で 行きます (〜 de ikimasu) (I go by [Transportation])  
Support chunks: 〜と 行きます / 行きません / 来ます / 帰ります  
Drill slots: school, station, work, home, bus, train, taxi, bicycle

## Phase 2: Build Everyday Conversation (Lessons 9-16)

Lesson 9:  
I can invite someone and suggest doing something together  
1. 〜ませんか (〜masen ka) (Won't you [Verb]?)  
2. 〜ましょう (〜mashou) (Let's [Verb])  
Support chunks: いいですね / ええ、ぜひ / すみません、ちょっと  
Drill slots: eat, drink, go, study, rest, talk, take a walk

Lesson 10:  
I can describe things and places with adjectives  
1. 〜は [i-Adjective]いです (〜 wa [i-Adjective]i desu) ([Subject] is [i-Adjective])  
2. 〜は [na-Adjective]です (〜 wa [na-Adjective] desu) ([Subject] is [na-Adjective])  
Support chunks: とても / あまり / [i-Adjective]くないです / [na-Adjective]じゃありません  
Drill slots: big, small, good, expensive, cheap, quiet, famous, convenient

Lesson 11:  
I can talk about what I like and what I am good at  
1. 〜が 好きです (〜 ga suki desu) (I like [Noun])  
2. 〜が 上手です (〜 ga jouzu desu) (I am good at [Noun])  
Support chunks: きらいです / 下手です / わかります  
Drill slots: music, sports, movies, cooking, Japanese, math, cats, coffee

Lesson 12:  
I can say what I want and what I want to do  
1. 〜が ほしいです (〜 ga hoshii desu) (I want [Noun])  
2. 〜たいです (〜tai desu) (I want to [Verb])  
Support chunks: あまり 〜たくないです / いまは 〜たくないです  
Drill slots: water, coffee, time, money, go, eat, buy, rest, sleep

Lesson 13:  
I can talk about what I did and did not do  
1. 〜ました (〜mashita) (I [Verb]ed)  
2. 〜ませんでした (〜masen deshita) (I did not [Verb])  
Support chunks: きのう / せんしゅう / それから  
Drill slots: went, ate, drank, studied, watched, bought, met, worked

Lesson 14:  
I can say how something was and explain why  
1. 〜でした / 〜かったです (〜deshita / 〜katta desu) (It was [Noun / na-Adjective / i-Adjective])  
2. 〜からです (〜 kara desu) (It is because [Reason])  
Support chunks: どうして 〜ですか / どうでしたか  
Drill slots: fun, busy, tired, quiet, good, bad, class, trip, restaurant

Lesson 15:  
I can compare things and say what is best  
1. Aより Bの ほうが 〜です (A yori B no hou ga 〜 desu) (B is more [Adjective] than A)  
2. 〜の 中で Aが いちばん 〜です (〜 no naka de A ga ichiban 〜 desu) (Among [Group], A is the most [Adjective])  
Support chunks: AとBと どちらが 〜ですか  
Drill slots: cities, foods, stores, apps, classes, hobbies, seasons

Lesson 16:  
I can say the purpose of going somewhere  
1. 〜へ [Verb Stem]に 行きます (〜 e [Verb Stem] ni ikimasu) (I go to [Place] to [Verb])  
2. 〜へ [Noun]に 行きます (〜 e [Noun] ni ikimasu) (I go to [Place] for [Noun])  
Support chunks: だれと 行きますか / 何を しに 行きますか  
Drill slots: eat, shop, study, see a friend, have coffee, take pictures, work

## Phase 3: Te-Form as the Conversation Engine (Lessons 17-24)

Lesson 17:  
I can connect actions and describe simple sequences  
1. 〜て、〜ます (〜te, 〜masu) (I [Verb], and then [Verb])  
2. 〜てから、〜ます (〜te kara, 〜masu) (After I [Verb], I [Verb])  
Support chunks: それから / それで  
Drill slots: wake up, brush teeth, eat, go, come home, shower, sleep

Lesson 18:  
I can make requests and state what is not allowed  
1. 〜て ください (〜te kudasai) (Please [Verb])  
2. 〜ては いけません (〜te wa ikemasen) (You must not [Verb])  
Support chunks: だめです / 図書館で / 教室で / 電車で  
Drill slots: wait, write, say, open, close, sit, smoke, eat, use phone

Lesson 19:  
I can ask for permission and describe ongoing actions  
1. 〜ても いいですか (〜te mo ii desu ka) (May I [Verb]?)  
2. 〜て います (〜te imasu) (I am [Verb]ing / It is in that state)  
Support chunks: はい、いいです / すみません、ちょっと / 〜に 住んでいます  
Drill slots: take a picture, use this, sit here, open the window, eating, studying, waiting

Lesson 20:  
I can say not yet and connect descriptions naturally  
1. まだ 〜て いません (mada 〜te imasen) (I have not [Verb]ed yet)  
2. 〜くて / 〜で、〜です (〜kute / 〜de, 〜 desu) ([It] is [Adjective / Noun], and [Adjective / Noun])  
Support chunks: 小さくて かわいいです / しずかで きれいです  
Drill slots: eat, finish, decide, send, small, big, quiet, clean, famous, convenient

Lesson 21:  
I can switch into casual plain forms for everyday talk  
1. [辞書形 / ない形] ([Dictionary Form / nai-Form]) ([Casual Present / Future / Negative])  
2. [た形 / なかった形] ([ta-Form / nakatta-Form]) ([Casual Past / Negative Past])  
Support chunks: うん / ううん / そうだね / ほんとう？  
Drill slots: go, eat, see, buy, do, come, read, meet

Lesson 22:  
I can say what I think and report what someone said  
1. [Plain Form] + と 思います ([Plain Form] + to omoimasu) (I think that [Clause])  
2. [Plain Form] + と 言って います ([Plain Form] + to itte imasu) ([Someone] says / is saying that [Clause])  
Support chunks: たぶん / ほんとうに / みんな  
Drill slots: fun, difficult, good, wants to go, is busy, will come, cannot do

Lesson 23:  
I can talk about ability and experience  
1. 〜ことが できます (〜koto ga dekimasu) (I can / am able to [Verb])  
2. 〜た ことが あります (〜ta koto ga arimasu) (I have done [Verb] before)  
Support chunks: 一度も ありません / どのくらい できますか  
Drill slots: speak, read, cook, swim, drive, travel, try, meet, eat

Lesson 24:  
I can give advice and talk about obligations  
1. 〜た ほうが いいです (〜ta hou ga ii desu) (You should [Verb])  
2. 〜なければ なりません (〜nakereba narimasen) (I must [Verb])  
Support chunks: 〜ない ほうが いいです / 〜なくても いいです  
Drill slots: sleep, study, go to the doctor, call, hurry, bring, wear, rest

## Phase 4: Expand Into Flexible Real-Life Speaking (Lessons 25-32)

Lesson 25:  
I can list examples and describe doing more than one kind of action  
1. 〜たり、〜たり します (〜tari, 〜tari shimasu) (I do things like [Verb] and [Verb])  
2. 〜ながら (〜nagara) (While [Verb]ing, [Action])  
Support chunks: 〜や 〜など / ときどき / しゅうまつは  
Drill slots: eat, study, watch, listen, walk, cook, talk, work

Lesson 26:  
I can talk about giving, receiving, and help between people  
1. 〜に 〜を あげます / もらいます (〜 ni 〜 o agemasu / moraimasu) (I give [Item] to / receive [Item] from [Person])  
2. 〜て くれます / 〜て もらいます (〜te kuremasu / 〜te moraimasu) ([Someone] does [Action] for me / I have [Someone] do [Action] for me)  
Support chunks: 〜が 〜を くれます  
Drill slots: book, gift, money, help, teach, wait, carry, explain

Lesson 27:  
I can describe nouns with longer information  
1. [Plain Sentence] + 名詞 ([Plain Sentence] + [Noun]) (the [Noun] that [Clause])  
2. 〜という + 名詞 (〜 to iu + [Noun]) (the [Noun] called / named 〜)  
Support chunks: わたしが 買った 本 / 日本に 住んでいる 友だち  
Drill slots: person, book, place, movie, shop, teacher, app, restaurant

Lesson 28:  
I can explain a situation and soften a request or problem  
1. 〜んです (〜n desu) (The thing is [Explanation])  
2. 〜んですが (〜n desu ga) (The thing is [Situation], and...)  
Support chunks: どうしたんですか / すみません、ちょっと  
Drill slots: late, busy, sick, looking for something, need help, cannot go

Lesson 29:  
I can talk about changes, decisions, and future plans  
1. 〜く / に なります (〜ku / ni narimasu) (It becomes [Adjective / Noun])  
2. 〜つもりです / 〜予定です (〜tsumori desu / 〜yotei desu) (I intend to [Verb] / It is planned)  
Support chunks: 〜に します (I will choose [Noun])  
Drill slots: warm, cold, busy, free, teacher, engineer, travel, move, study

Lesson 30:  
I can try things and prepare in advance  
1. 〜て みます (〜te mimasu) (I will try [Verb]ing)  
2. 〜て おきます (〜te okimasu) (I will [Verb] in advance / for later)  
Support chunks: もう 〜て あります  
Drill slots: eat, use, ask, check, call, reserve, write down, buy

Lesson 31:  
I can talk about conditions and likely results  
1. 〜たら、〜 (〜tara, 〜) (If / When [Condition], [Result])  
2. 〜と、〜 (〜to, 〜) (When [Condition], [Natural Result])  
Support chunks: 〜ても、〜 / もし 〜たら  
Drill slots: go, arrive, press, eat, study, have time, be free, rain

Lesson 32:  
I can make soft guesses and report information  
1. 〜かもしれません (〜kamo shiremasen) (It might be [Clause])  
2. [Casual Sentence] + そうです ([Casual Sentence] + sou desu) (I heard that [Clause])  
Support chunks: たぶん / 〜でしょう  
Drill slots: weather, plans, delays, prices, schedules, health, rumors, events

## Suggested Recycling Logic

- Lessons 1-8: identity, objects, places, time, daily life
- Lessons 9-16: opinions, wants, comparison, past, reasons, going to do things
- Lessons 17-24: te-form control, casual form access, ability, experience, advice
- Lessons 25-32: longer turns, favors, explanation, plans, conditions, uncertainty

## Teaching Notes for Each Lesson

1. Start with a **model dialogue** using only old material plus the new pattern.  
2. Run **slot substitution drills** first.  
3. Move to **Q&A drills** with fast turns.  
4. End with a **30-60 second pair task** where learners must recycle at least one old pattern and the new one.  
5. Keep vocabulary narrow. The lesson target is the **pattern**, not the word list.
