Where Is It? — ここkoko・そこsoko・あそこasoko

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN
In this lesson, you will learn how to:
• Ask where something is
• Say "here," "there," and "over there"
• Answer "Where is ___?" questions

THE BIG IDEA: ここkoko・そこsoko・あそこasoko

English Explanation

In Lesson 2 you learned これkore・それsore・あれare for pointing at things.

Now we learn the place versions — same distance logic!

• ここkoko = here — near me

• そこsoko = there — near you

• あそこasoko = over there — far from both of us

PATTERN 1: ASKING WHERE

"Where is the ___?"

Pattern: [noun]はwaどこdokoですdesuかka？

How to use

Put the thing you're looking for before はwa, then say どこdokoですdesuかka？ (where is it?)

どこdoko = where

EXAMPLES

• ほんhonはwaどこdokoですdesuかka？
Where is the book?

• ペンpenはwaどこdokoですdesuかka？
Where is the pen?

• かばんkabanはwaどこdokoですdesuかka？
Where is the bag?

PATTERN 2: ANSWERING WHERE

"The ___ is here / there / over there."

Pattern: [noun]はwa ここkoko/そこsoko/あそこasoko ですdesu。

How to use

When someone asks どこdokoですdesuかka？, answer with the location word that matches the distance:

• ここkoko = here (near me, the speaker)

• そこsoko = there (near you, the listener)

• あそこasoko = over there (far from both of us)

EXAMPLES

• ほんhonはwaここkokoですdesu。
The book is here.

• ペンpenはwaそこsokoですdesu。
The pen is there.

• かばんkabanはwaあそこasokoですdesu。
The bag is over there.
