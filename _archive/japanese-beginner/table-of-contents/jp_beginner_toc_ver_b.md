Lesson 1:  
I can identify things and talk about who they belong to  
1\. これは \~です (kore wa \~desu) (This is \[Object\])  
2\. これは \~の \~です (kore wa \~no \~desu) (This is \[Someone's\] \[Object\])

Lesson 2:  
I can ask and say where places and objects are located  
1\. ここは \~です (koko wa \~desu) (Here is \[Place\])  
2\. \~は どこですか (\~wa doko desu ka) (Where is \[Place/Object\]?)

Lesson 3:  
I can ask for prices and order items while shopping  
1\. \~は いくらですか (\~wa ikura desu ka) (How much is \[Item\]?)  
2\. \~を ください (\~o kudasai) (Please give me \[Item\])

Lesson 4:  
I can talk about my daily habits and things I don't do  
1\. \~を します (\~o shimasu) (I do/will do \[Action\])  
2\. \~を しません (\~o shimasen) (I do not/will not do \[Action\])

Lesson 5:  
I can talk about timeframes and starting/ending points  
1\. \~時から \~時まで (\~ji kara \~ji made) (From \[Time\] to \[Time\])  
2\. \~から \~まで (\~kara \~made) (From \[Place/Point A\] to \[Place/Point B\])

Lesson 6:  
I can talk about where I am going and how I get there  
1\. \~に 行きます (\~ni ikimasu) (I go/will go to \[Destination\])  
2\. \~で \~に 行きます (\~de \~ni ikimasu) (I go to \[Destination\] by \[Transportation\])

Lesson 7:  
I can talk about what I did and didn't do in the past  
1\. \~ました (\~mashita) (I did \[Action\])  
2\. \~ませんでした (\~masen deshita) (I did not do \[Action\])

Lesson 8:  
I can describe what is in a room or location  
1\. \~に \~が あります (\~ni \~ga arimasu) (There is an \[Inanimate Object\] at \[Location\])  
2\. \~に \~が います (\~ni \~ga imasu) (There is a \[Person/Animal\] at \[Location\])

Lesson 9:  
I can describe the characteristics of things and places  
1\. \~は \[い-Adj\]いです (\~wa \[i-Adj\]i desu) (\[Subject\] is \[i-Adjective\])  
2\. \~は \[な-Adj\]です (\~wa \[na-Adj\] desu) (\[Subject\] is \[na-Adjective\])

Lesson 10:  
I can talk about what I like and what I am good at  
1\. \~が 好きです (\~ga suki desu) (I like \[Noun\])  
2\. \~が 上手です (\~ga jouzu desu) (I am good at \[Noun\])

Lesson 11:  
I can invite friends to do things together  
1\. \~を しませんか (\~o shimasen ka) (Won't you do \[Action\] with me?)  
2\. \~を しましょう (\~o shimashou) (Let's do \[Action\])

Lesson 12:  
I can ask someone to do or not do something  
1\. \~て ください (\~te kudasai) (Please do \[Action\])  
2\. \~ないで ください (\~naide kudasai) (Please do not do \[Action\])

Lesson 13:  
I can describe ongoing actions and current states  
1\. \~て います (\~te imasu) (I am currently doing \[Action\] / State of being)  
2\. \~て いません (\~te imasen) (I am not currently doing \[Action\] / Not in state)

Lesson 14:  
I can ask for permission and talk about rules  
1\. \~ても いいですか (\~te mo ii desu ka) (May I do \[Action\]?)  
2\. \~ては いけません (\~te wa ikemasen) (You must not do \[Action\])

Lesson 15:  
I can connect multiple actions and descriptions  
1\. \~て、\~ (\~te, \~) (I do \[Action 1\], and then \[Action 2\])  
2\. \~くて / \~で、\~ (\~kute / \~de, \~) (\[Adj/Noun 1\], and \[Adj/Noun 2\])

Lesson 16:  
I can give reasons for things  
1\. \~から、\~ (\~kara, \~) (Because \[Reason\], \[Result\] \- Direct)  
2\. \~ので、\~ (\~node, \~) (Because \[Reason\], \[Result\] \- Polite/Objective)

Lesson 17:  
I can talk about giving and receiving items  
1\. \~に \~を あげます (\~ni \~o agemasu) (I give \[Item\] to \[Someone\])  
2\. \~に \~を もらいます (\~ni \~o moraimasu) (I receive \[Item\] from \[Someone\])

Lesson 18:  
I can compare two or more things  
1\. Aより Bのほうが \~です (A yori B no hou ga \~desu) (B is more \[Adjective\] than A)  
2\. \~の中で \~が いちばん \~です (\~no naka de \~ga ichiban \~desu) (Among \~, \[Subject\] is the most \[Adjective\])

Lesson 19:  
I can express what I want and what I want to do  
1\. \~が ほしいです (\~ga hoshii desu) (I want \[Noun\])  
2\. \~たいです (\~tai desu) (I want to do \[Action\])

Lesson 20:  
I can talk about my future plans and intentions  
1\. \~つもりです (\~tsumori desu) (I intend/plan to do \[Action\])  
2\. \~予定です (\~yotei desu) (It is scheduled that / I plan to \[Noun/Action\])

Lesson 21:  
I can talk about my life experiences and give advice  
1\. \~た ことが あります (\~ta koto ga arimasu) (I have the experience of doing \[Action\])  
2\. \~た ほうが いいです (\~ta hou ga ii desu) (It is better to do / You should do \[Action\])

Lesson 22:  
I can list multiple items and examples  
1\. \~や \~など (\~ya \~nado) (Things like \[Noun A\] and \[Noun B\])  
2\. \~とか \~とか (\~toka \~toka) (Things like \[Noun A\] and \[Noun B\] \- Casual)

Lesson 23:  
I can list multiple actions without being exhaustive  
1\. \~たり、\~たり します (\~tari, \~tari shimasu) (I do things like \[Action A\] and \[Action B\])  
2\. \~たり、\~たり しました (\~tari, \~tari shimashita) (I did things like \[Action A\] and \[Action B\])

Lesson 24:  
I can speak casually with friends and family  
1\. \[辞書形/ない形\] (\[Dictionary Form/nai-Form\]) (\[Action\] \- Casual Present/Future)  
2\. \[た形/なかった形\] (\[ta-Form/nakatta-Form\]) (\[Action\] \- Casual Past)

Lesson 25:  
I can describe nouns using full sentences (Relative Clauses)  
1\. \[Verb \- Present Plain\] \~Noun (\[Noun\] that \[Actions/Is Actioned\])  
2\. \[Verb \- Past Plain\] \~Noun (\[Noun\] that \[Actioned/Was Actioned\])

Lesson 26:  
I can describe how things change over time  
1\. \~く なります (\~ku narimasu) (It becomes \[i-Adjective\])  
2\. \~に なります (\~ni narimasu) (It becomes \[na-Adjective/Noun\])

Lesson 27:  
I can express my opinions and tell people what others said  
1\. \~と 思います (\~to omoimasu) (I think that \[Clause\])  
2\. \~と 言いました (\~to iimashita) (\[Someone\] said that \[Clause\])

Lesson 28:  
I can talk about what I must do and what I don't need to do  
1\. \~なければ なりません (\~nakereba narimasen) (I must do \[Action\])  
2\. \~なくても いいです (\~nakutemo ii desu) (I do not have to do \[Action\])

Lesson 29:  
I can talk about actions done in preparation or left in a certain state  
1\. \~て あります (\~te arimasu) (\[Action\] has been done / is left in that state)  
2\. \~て おきます (\~te okimasu) (I will do \[Action\] in advance/preparation)

Lesson 30:  
I can express my abilities and what is possible  
1\. \~が できます (\~ga dekimasu) (I am capable of / can play \[Noun\])  
2\. \~ことが できます (\~koto ga dekimasu) (I am able to do \[Action\])

Lesson 31:  
I can talk about hypothetical situations and conditions  
1\. \~たら、\~ (\~tara, \~) (If/When \[Condition\], \[Result\])  
2\. \~ても、\~ (\~temo, \~) (Even if \[Condition\], \[Result\])

Lesson 32:  
I can describe doing things simultaneously and going somewhere with a purpose  
1\. \~ながら、\~ (\~nagara, \~) (While doing \[Action 1\], I do \[Action 2\])  
2\. \~に 行きます (\~ni ikimasu) (I am going in order to do \[Action\])

Lesson 33:  
I can describe how things look and talk about doing things excessively  
1\. \~そうです (\~sou desu) (It looks like \[Adjective/Action\])  
2\. \~すぎます (\~sugimasu) (I do \[Action\] too much / It is too \[Adjective\])

Lesson 34:  
I can express my intentions and talk about trying new things  
1\. \~ようと 思います (\~you to omoimasu) (I am thinking of doing \[Action\])  
2\. \~て みます (\~te mimasu) (I will try doing \[Action\] to see how it goes)

Lesson 35:  
I can talk about doing favors for others and receiving help  
1\. \~て くれます (\~te kuremasu) (\[Someone\] kindly does \[Action\] for me)  
2\. \~て もらいます (\~te moraimasu) (I have \[Someone\] do \[Action\] for me)

Lesson 36:  
I can describe how easy or difficult something is to do  
1\. \~やすいです (\~yasui desu) (It is easy to do \[Action\])  
2\. \~にくいです (\~nikui desu) (It is difficult to do \[Action\])

Lesson 37:  
I can express strong expectations and feelings of disappointment  
1\. \~はずです (\~hazu desu) (It is expected that / I am sure that \[Clause\])  
2\. \~のに、\~ (\~noni, \~) (Even though \[Condition\], \[Unexpected Result\])

Lesson 38:  
I can pass on information I heard and make guesses  
1\. \~そうです (\~sou desu) (I heard that \[Clause\])  
2\. \~かもしれません (\~kamo shiremasen) (It might be that / Perhaps \[Clause\])

Lesson 39:  
I can describe the sequence of events using before and after  
1\. \~前に (\~mae ni) (Before \[Action/Noun\])  
2\. \~あとで (\~ato de) (After \[Action/Noun\])

Lesson 40:  
I can talk about completing an action entirely or doing something by accident  
1\. \~て しまいます (\~te shimaimasu) (I will completely finish \[Action\])  
2\. \~て しまいました (\~te shimaimashita) (I accidentally/regrettably did \[Action\])

Lesson 41:  
I can talk about natural consequences and necessary conditions  
1\. \~と、\~ (\~to, \~) (Whenever / If \[Action happens\], \[Natural Consequence\])  
2\. \~ば、\~ (\~ba, \~) (If \[Condition is met\], \[Good Result\])

Lesson 42:  
I can describe actions done to me by others and how things are made  
1\. \~に \~を されます (\~ni \~o saremasu) (I have my \[Object\] \[Action-ed\] by \[Someone\])  
2\. \~によって \~られます (\~ni yotte \~raremasu) (\[Subject\] is \[Action-ed\] by \[Someone\])

Lesson 43:  
I can embed questions into my other sentences  
1\. \~か どうか、\~ (\~ka dou ka, \~) (Whether or not \[Clause\], \[Result/Action\])  
2\. \[Question Word\] \~か、\~ (\[Question Word\] \~ka, \~) (What/Who/Where \[Clause\], \[Result/Action\])

Lesson 44:  
I can give advice and make gentle suggestions  
1\. \~たら どうですか (\~tara dou desu ka) (How about doing \[Action\]?)  
2\. \~ば いいです (\~ba ii desu) (You should just do / It would be good if you do \[Action\])

Lesson 45:  
I can talk about making or allowing someone to do something  
1\. \~に \~を させます (\~ni \~o sasemasu) (I make/let \[Someone\] do \[Action\])  
2\. \~させて ください (\~sasete kudasai) (Please allow me to do \[Action\])
