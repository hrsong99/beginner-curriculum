PRESTUDY: 〜をo 〜ますmasu

What Do You Eat, Drink, Read?

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN
In this lesson, you will learn how to:
• Pattern A: [noun]をo [verb]ますmasu — say what you do, ask, and say what you don't
• Pattern B: [noun]をo [verb]ませんかmasenka？ — invite someone ("Shall we…?")

THE BIG IDEA: をo MARKS THE THING

English Explanation

In English we say "I eat rice." The word rice is the thing we are acting on.

In Japanese, you mark that thing with the particle をo (pronounced "o").

• ごはんgohanをoたべますtabemasu。 — I eat rice.

• コーヒーkōhīをoのみますnomimasu。 — I drink coffee.

Quick Rule

Put the thing first, then をo, then the verb.

Japanese verbs come at the end of the sentence.

PATTERN A: 〜をo 〜ますmasu

I do / Do you…? / I don't

Pattern: [noun]をo [verb]ますmasu。

Three endings, one pattern

• 〜ますmasu — I do it (positive)

• 〜ますかmasuka？ — Do you…? (question — just add かka)

• 〜ませんmasen — I don't (negative)

EXAMPLES

• ごはんgohanをoたべますtabemasu。
I eat rice.

• コーヒーkōhīをoのみますnomimasuかka？
Do you drink coffee?

• テレビterebiをoみませんmimasen。
I don't watch TV.

• ほんhonをoよみますyomimasuかka？
Do you read books?

• おんがくongakuをoききますkikimasu。
I listen to music.

PATTERN B: 〜ませんかmasenka？

Shall we…? / Would you like…?

Pattern: [noun]をo [verb]ませんかmasenka？

How to use

You already know 〜ませんmasen means "I don't…" (from Pattern A).

When you add かka to the end, it becomes a friendly invitation — like "Shall we…?" or "Would you like to…?"

Same ending, but a totally different feeling — it's a warm offer, not a "no"!

COMPARE: 〜ませんmasen vs 〜ませんかmasenka？

〜ませんmasen (negative) 〜ませんかmasenka？ (invitation)
コーヒーkōhīをoのみませんnomimasen。
I don't drink coffee. 		コーヒーkōhīをoのみませんかnomimasenka？
Shall we drink coffee?
えいがeigaをoみませんmimasen。
I don't watch movies. 		えいがeigaをoみませんかmimasenka？
Shall we watch a movie?

MORE EXAMPLES

• パンpanをoたべませんかtabemasenka？
Would you like some bread?

• おんがくongakuをoききませんかkikimasenka？
Shall we listen to music?

• ほんhonをoよみませんかyomimasenka？
Shall we read a book?
Replying to an invitation

You'll hear these in class — you don't need to memorize them, just recognize them:

• はいhai、いいですねii desu ne。 — Yes, sounds good!

• すみませんsumimasen、ちょっとchotto… — Sorry, a little… (polite "no")

CONVERSATION PREVIEW

CONTEXT / じょうきょうjōkyō
Alex and Yuki are at a cafe ordering lunch.
アレックスArekkusuとゆきYukiがカフェkafeでひるごはんhirugohanをちゅうもんchūmonします。
How to read this

Read through the conversation slowly. The words in italics are the patterns you are learning. English translations are below each line.

YUKI: アレックスArekkusuさん、なにnaniをoのみますかnomimasuka？
Alex, what will you drink?

ALEX: わたしwatashiはコーヒーkōhīをoのみますnomimasu。
I'll drink coffee.

YUKI: いっしょisshoにパンpanをoたべませんかtabemasenka？
Shall we eat bread together?

ALEX: はいhai、いいですねii desu ne！ゆきYukiさんはなにnaniをoたべますかtabemasuka？
Yes, sounds good! What will you eat, Yuki?

YUKI: わたしwatashiはごはんgohanをoたべますtabemasu。みずmizuものみますnomimasu。
I'll eat rice. I'll drink water too.

ALEX: いいですねii desu ne！
Sounds good!

Notice

• のみますnomimasu / たべますtabemasu — Pattern A positive

• のみますかnomimasuka / たべますかtabemasuka — Pattern A question

• たべませんかtabemasenka — Pattern B invitation!

TRAVEL IN JAPAN: AT A RESTAURANT

Imagine you're at a small Japanese restaurant. Read the conversation out loud!

CONTEXT / じょうきょうjōkyō
The waiter is taking your order at a small Japanese
restaurant. Tell them what you'll eat and drink.
NEW WORDS
すしsushi (sushi)   おちゃocha (green tea)
メニューmenyū (menu)   おねがいしますonegaishimasu (please)
いらっしゃいませirasshaimase (welcome!)

WAITER: いらっしゃいませirasshaimase！なにnaniをoたべますかtabemasuka？
Welcome! What will you eat?

YOU: わたしwatashiはすしsushiをoたべますtabemasu。
I'll have sushi.

WAITER: なにnaniをoのみますかnomimasuka？
What would you like to drink?

YOU: おちゃochaをoのみますnomimasu。
I'll have green tea.

WAITER: パンpanもたべますかtabemasuka？
Will you also eat bread?

YOU: いいえiie、パンpanはたべませんtabemasen。
No, I won't eat bread.

YOU: おねがいしますonegaishimasu！
Please (please bring it)!

Notice

• たべますtabemasu / のみますnomimasu — "I will eat / drink"

• たべますかtabemasuka / のみますかnomimasuka — "Will you eat / drink?"

• たべませんtabemasen — "I won't eat" (polite refusal)
