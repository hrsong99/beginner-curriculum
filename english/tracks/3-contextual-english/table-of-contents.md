# Contextual English — Table of Contents

**48 lessons · 96 trainable patterns.** This track turns Core language into successful action in four high-value domains: everyday life, travel, relationships, and work.

> **Two-pattern rule:** every lesson contains two model lines that belong in the same exchange. One or two fixed expressions may support the scene, but do not become extra teaching targets. Context creates the need; the learner extracts the frames and reuses them with new details.

Each course names a Core prerequisite. Tutors may preview a fixed phrase for immediate survival, but they should not explain or drill untaught grammar as a hidden third lesson.

# Everyday English

## Course 1 · Getting Around Town [A1] · 6 lessons

**Prerequisite:** Core 1–19

**1. Find a place**
- “Excuse me, where’s the nearest restroom?” — `where's the nearest + place?`
- “It’s next to the pharmacy.” — `it's + place preposition + landmark`
- *Expressions:* `excuse me` · `near here`

**2. Follow directions**
- “Go straight and turn right at the light.” — `go/turn + direction + at + landmark`
- “Is it before or after the bank?” — `is it before or after + landmark?`
- *Expressions:* `on your left` · `you can't miss it`

**3. Choose transport**
- “What’s the best way to get downtown?” — `what's the best way to get to + place?`
- “The subway is faster than the bus.” — `A is + comparative + than B`
- *Expressions:* `rush hour` · `walking distance`

**4. Use public transit**
- “Does this train go to City Hall?” — `does this + transport + go to + place?`
- “You need to transfer at Central.” — `you need to + transport action + at + place`
- *Expressions:* `one-way ticket` · `next stop`

**5. Fix a route mistake**
- “I think I got on the wrong bus.” — `I think I + past mistake`
- “Where should I get off?” — `where should I + base verb?`
- *Expressions:* `wrong direction` · `go back`

**6. Meet at the right place**
- “I’m waiting outside Exit Two.” — `I'm waiting + location`
- “I’ll be there in about ten minutes.” — `I'll be there in about + duration`
- *Expressions:* `running late` · `see you soon`

## Course 2 · Food, Shopping & Home [A1–A2] · 6 lessons

**Prerequisite:** Core 14–35

**1. Find an item**
- “Where can I find the rice?” — `where can I find + item?`
- “Do you have this in a smaller size?” — `do you have this in + option?`
- *Expressions:* `in stock` · `over there`

**2. Order at a café**
- “I’d like a large iced coffee.” — `I'd like + item`
- “Can I get it without sugar?” — `can I get it with/without + item?`
- *Expressions:* `for here` · `to go`

**3. Ask about a menu**
- “What do you recommend?” — `what do you recommend?`
- “Does this contain any nuts?” — `does this contain + ingredient?`
- *Expressions:* `house special` · `allergic to`

**4. Change or return something**
- “This shirt is too small for me.” — `item + be + too + adjective + for + person`
- “Could I exchange it for a larger one?” — `could I exchange A for B?`
- *Expressions:* `receipt` · `store credit`

**5. Report a home problem**
- “The heater isn’t working.” — `item + isn't working`
- “Could you send someone to look at it?” — `could you send someone to + base verb?`
- *Expressions:* `as soon as possible` · `maintenance`

**6. Arrange a service**
- “I need to get my phone repaired.” — `need to get + object + past participle`
- “When will it be ready?” — `when will + item + be ready?`
- *Expressions:* `service fee` · `pick it up`

# Travel English

## Course 3 · Airport & Border [A2] · 6 lessons

**Prerequisite:** Core 25–46

**1. Check in for a flight**
- “I’d like to check in for Flight 208.” — `I'd like to check in for + flight`
- “Could I have an aisle seat?” — `could I have + seat/request?`
- *Expressions:* `boarding pass` · `carry-on`

**2. Go through security**
- “Do I have to take my laptop out?” — `do I have to + base verb?`
- “Liquids must be placed in this bag.” — `item + must be + past participle`
- *Expressions:* `security tray` · `empty your pockets`

**3. Handle a delay**
- “Has the departure time changed?” — `has + item + past participle?`
- “Do you know when boarding will start?” — `do you know when + clause?`
- *Expressions:* `delayed` · `gate change`

**4. Answer immigration questions**
- “I’m here for a two-week vacation.” — `I'm here for + purpose/duration`
- “I’ll be staying at the Grand Hotel.” — `I'll be staying at + place`
- *Expressions:* `return ticket` · `anything to declare`

**5. Find missing baggage**
- “My suitcase hasn’t arrived.” — `item + hasn't + past participle`
- “It may be on the wrong belt.” — `may/might be + place`
- *Expressions:* `baggage claim` · `tracking number`

**6. Make a connection**
- “Will I have enough time to make my connection?” — `will I have enough time to + base verb?`
- “If you hurry, you’ll make it.” — `if + present, will + base verb`
- *Expressions:* `connecting flight` · `final call`

## Course 4 · Hotel & Travel Problems [A2–B1] · 6 lessons

**Prerequisite:** Core 25–53

**1. Check in**
- “I have a reservation under Kim.” — `have a reservation under + name`
- “Could you tell me what time breakfast starts?” — `could you tell me + indirect question?`
- *Expressions:* `photo ID` · `room key`

**2. Ask for room items**
- “Could you bring up two more towels?” — `could you bring up + quantity + item?`
- “Would you mind leaving them by the door?” — `would you mind + verb-ing + place?`
- *Expressions:* `extra blanket` · `housekeeping`

**3. Explain a malfunction**
- “The air conditioner won’t turn on.” — `item + won't + base verb`
- “Have you tried restarting it?” — `have you tried + verb-ing?`
- *Expressions:* `control panel` · `power switch`

**4. Deal with noise**
- “There’s been a lot of noise next door.” — `there has been + problem + location`
- “Is there any chance I could change rooms?” — `is there any chance I could + base verb?`
- *Expressions:* `keep it down` · `quiet room`

**5. Ask about a lost item**
- “I may have left my wallet in the lobby.” — `may/might have left + object + place`
- “Has anything been turned in?” — `has anything been + past participle?`
- *Expressions:* `lost and found` · `describe the item`

**6. Check out and store bags**
- “Could I leave my bags here until three?” — `could I leave + object + place + until + time?`
- “I’d like to check out, please.” — `I'd like to + service action`
- *Expressions:* `late checkout` · `luggage tag`

# Social English

## Course 5 · Meeting People & Making Plans [A1–A2] · 6 lessons

**Prerequisite:** Core 7–49

**1. Start a conversation**
- “How do you know Maya?” — `how do you know + person?`
- “We worked together at my last job.” — `past action + finished-time context`
- *Expressions:* `by the way` · `small world`

**2. Find common interests**
- “What do you do for fun?” — `what do you do for fun?`
- “I’m really into live music.” — `be into + noun/verb-ing`
- *Expressions:* `me too` · `what kind of...?`

**3. Invite someone**
- “Would you like to get coffee sometime?” — `would you like to + base verb?`
- “I’d love to.” — `I'd love to / I'd be happy to`
- *Expressions:* `sounds good` · `sometime`

**4. Choose a time**
- “How about Saturday afternoon?” — `how about + time/noun?`
- “I’m free after three.” — `be free + time expression`
- *Expressions:* `works for me` · `let me check`

**5. Decline without closing the door**
- “I’d love to, but I already have plans.” — `I'd love to, but + reason`
- “Could we do it another day?” — `could we + base verb + another time?`
- *Expressions:* `maybe next time` · `rain check`

**6. Follow up afterward**
- “I had a really good time.” — `had a + adjective + time`
- “We should do this again.” — `we should + base verb + again`
- *Expressions:* `thanks for coming` · `get home safely`

## Course 6 · Feelings, Advice & Conflict [A2–B1] · 6 lessons

**Prerequisite:** Core 35–56

**1. Catch up with a friend**
- “How have you been?” — `how have you been?`
- “I’ve been pretty busy lately.” — `I've been + state + lately`
- *Expressions:* `long time no see` · `what's new?`

**2. Share a feeling and its cause**
- “I’m nervous about the interview.” — `be + feeling + about + noun`
- “It’s because I really want the job.” — `it's because + reason`
- *Expressions:* `mixed feelings` · `stressed out`

**3. Ask for and give advice**
- “What would you do if you were me?” — `what would you do if + past?`
- “If I were you, I’d talk to her.” — `if I were you, I'd + base verb`
- *Expressions:* `sleep on it` · `trust your gut`

**4. Clear up a misunderstanding**
- “That’s not what I meant.” — `that's not what I meant`
- “I thought you said you were leaving.” — `I thought you said + reported clause`
- *Expressions:* `get the wrong idea` · `let me explain`

**5. Apologize and respond**
- “I’m sorry for interrupting you.” — `I'm sorry for + verb-ing`
- “It’s okay. I know you didn’t mean to.” — `I know you didn't mean to + base verb`
- *Expressions:* `my fault` · `no hard feelings`

**6. Disagree and compromise**
- “I see what you mean, but I feel differently.” — `I see what you mean, but + position`
- “Could we meet halfway?” — `could we + compromise action?`
- *Expressions:* `fair enough` · `agree to disagree`

# Work English

## Course 7 · Workplace Basics [A2] · 6 lessons

**Prerequisite:** Core 13–49

**1. Explain your role**
- “I’m responsible for customer support.” — `be responsible for + noun/verb-ing`
- “I mainly work with the sales team.” — `mainly work with/on + noun`
- *Expressions:* `day-to-day` · `report to`

**2. Describe current work**
- “I’m working on the monthly report.” — `be working on + project`
- “I’ve already finished the first section.” — `have already + past participle`
- *Expressions:* `in progress` · `almost done`

**3. Ask a colleague for help**
- “Could you take a look at this?” — `could you take a look at + item?`
- “Would you mind sending me the latest file?” — `would you mind + verb-ing + object?`
- *Expressions:* `when you have a minute` · `latest version`

**4. Schedule a call**
- “Are you available on Thursday afternoon?” — `are you available + time?`
- “Could we move it to Friday?” — `could we move + event + to + time?`
- *Expressions:* `calendar invite` · `time zone`

**5. Report a technical problem**
- “I can’t access the shared folder.” — `can't access + system/item`
- “It was working this morning.” — `was working + past time`
- *Expressions:* `permission error` · `log back in`

**6. Give a progress update**
- “We’ve completed three of the five tasks.” — `have completed + quantity + of + total`
- “We should be finished by Friday.” — `should be + past participle + by + deadline`
- *Expressions:* `on track` · `running behind`

## Course 8 · Meetings & Professional Problems [B1] · 6 lessons

**Prerequisite:** Core 39–56

**1. Open and manage a call**
- “I’m calling to ask about my order.” — `I'm calling to + purpose`
- “Could you put me through to billing?” — `could you put me through to + person/team?`
- *Expressions:* `hold the line` · `leave a message`

**2. State a meeting goal**
- “The purpose of today’s meeting is to choose a launch date.” — `the purpose of + event + is to + base verb`
- “Let’s start by reviewing the options.” — `let's start by + verb-ing`
- *Expressions:* `agenda` · `get started`

**3. Give and soften an opinion**
- “I think Option A is the safest.” — `I think + position`
- “You have a point, but it may cost too much.” — `concession + but + concern`
- *Expressions:* `from my perspective` · `I’m not convinced`

**4. Clarify and summarize**
- “Could you clarify what you mean by ‘ready’?” — `could you clarify what you mean by + term?`
- “So, what you’re saying is that we need more time.” — `what you're saying is that + clause`
- *Expressions:* `in other words` · `let me make sure`

**5. Give constructive feedback**
- “I like the overall direction, but this section needs more detail.” — `positive point + but + change`
- “Have you considered adding an example?” — `have you considered + verb-ing?`
- *Expressions:* `specific feedback` · `work on`

**6. Handle a complaint**
- “I understand why you’re frustrated.” — `I understand why + clause`
- “I’ll look into it and get back to you by noon.” — `I'll look into + issue + and get back by + time`
- *Expressions:* `sort it out` · `next steps`

## Contextual lesson performance rule

A learner passes a lesson by completing the situation with both patterns, not by reciting the model. Change at least two details—person, item, place, time, reason, or outcome—during the final role-play.
