Podo · English for Japanese speakers · for native review

Everything the curriculum asks a learner to say

303 items across three tracks, and none of it has been read by a native speaker yet. The sentences are set large because they are the thing being judged; frames, Core links and difficulty notes are context, sized to stay out of the way.

What to look for: would a native speaker actually say this, in this situation? Not whether it is grammatical — everything here is. Whether it is what a person reaches for.

How to flag it: every item has an id. Write CORE-31: nobody says this and it is unambiguous. A rejected sentence is not deleted — it moves to the expressions line, so the learner still meets it but is not asked to produce it.

Core Patterns

The main sequence, Pre-A1 to C1. Two frames per lesson, and they have to work together in one exchange. Lessons past 70 are Part 2 — written scene-first, but Korean's equivalent lost 28 of 120 patterns at this stage, so expect a cull rather than a pass.
