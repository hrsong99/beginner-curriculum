# English Main Curriculum

**Goal:** take an adult beginner from first usable sentences to independent everyday and workplace conversation, without separating grammar from what the learner is trying to do.

This folder adapts the teaching architecture in `korean/tracks/` to English. The central contract is the same:

> **One lesson, two reusable patterns.** Each pattern begins as a natural model sentence, is noticed in context, and is then changed and spoken by the learner. New language moves from receptive work to guided production to personal production.

## Track order

| [`3-contextual-english`](./tracks/3-contextual-english/table-of-contents.md) | Transfer known patterns into daily, travel, social, and work situations | 48 lessons · 96 patterns | Begin after Core Lesson 12; follow prerequisites |

The tracks are parallel after the opening stage. A learner does not need to “finish pronunciation” before speaking, and contextual lessons do not introduce grammar that the core spine has not prepared.

## Lesson contract

Every taught lesson has exactly two primary patterns. Vocabulary, expressions, and pronunciation notes may support them, but they do not become hidden third objectives.

1. **Hook:** hear or read a short situation containing both patterns.
2. **Notice Pattern A:** meaning first, then form and sound.
3. **Use Pattern A:** substitute, choose, and personalize.
4. **Notice Pattern B:** connect it to the same communicative goal.
5. **Use Pattern B:** substitute, choose, and personalize.
6. **Combine:** complete one realistic exchange using both.
7. **Retrieve:** close the model and say the exchange again.

See [`lesson-template.md`](./shared/lesson-template.md) for the authoring template and [`curriculum-rationale.md`](./reference/curriculum-rationale.md) for research decisions.

## Level promise

- **Pre-A1 / early A1:** supported exchanges about self, objects, needs, time, and place.
- **A1:** routine transactions and short personal conversations.
- **A2:** connected accounts of plans, experiences, comparisons, and problems.
- **B1:** independent interaction, reasons, stories, polite negotiation, and familiar workplace tasks.

CEFR labels are planning guides, not certification claims. Each level should ultimately be validated through recorded learner performances against explicit can-do tasks.
