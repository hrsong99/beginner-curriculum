# English Main Curriculum

**Goal:** take an adult beginner from first usable speaking frames to independent everyday and workplace conversation.

This folder adapts the teaching architecture in `korean/tracks/` to English. The central contract is the same:

> **One lesson, two reusable speaking patterns.** A pattern is a literal phrase frame such as `Could you ___?`, not a grammar formula such as “modal + subject + verb.” The learner hears it in context, changes its slots, and retrieves it in conversation.

## Track order

| Track | Job | Size | Recommended point of use |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| [`1-core-patterns`](./tracks/1-core-patterns/table-of-contents.md) | Build the main inventory of reusable speaking frames | 72 lessons · 144 patterns | Main sequence, Pre-A1 to B1 |
| [`2-contextual-english`](./tracks/2-contextual-english/table-of-contents.md) | Transfer known patterns into daily, travel, social, and work situations | 48 lessons · 96 patterns | Begin after Core Lesson 18; follow per-lesson prerequisites |
| [`3-freetalking`](./tracks/3-freetalking/table-of-contents.md) | Develop opinions, interaction, and fluency with recycled language | 24 launch topics · growing | Begin around Core Lesson 37 |

The tracks become parallel after the opening stage. Core introduces frames in small exchanges; Contextual English combines them to complete larger situations; Freetalking retrieves them while the learner expresses genuine ideas.

## Lesson contract

Every taught lesson has exactly two primary patterns. Each one must retain literal words and no more than two useful slots. Vocabulary, expressions, grammar explanations, and optional sound cues may support the frames, but they do not become hidden third objectives.

1. **Hook:** hear or read a short situation containing both patterns.
2. **Notice Pattern A:** meaning first, then stable words and changeable slots.
3. **Use Pattern A:** substitute, choose, and personalize.
4. **Notice Pattern B:** connect it as an answer, response, follow-up, repair, or contrast.
5. **Use Pattern B:** substitute, choose, and personalize.
6. **Combine:** complete one realistic exchange using both.
7. **Retrieve:** close the model and say the exchange again.

Grammar has not been removed; it has moved backstage. See [`grammar-coverage-map.md`](./reference/grammar-coverage-map.md) for sequencing and coverage, [`lesson-template.md`](./shared/lesson-template.md) for authoring, and [`curriculum-rationale.md`](./reference/curriculum-rationale.md) for research decisions.

## Level promise

- **Pre-A1 / early A1:** supported exchanges about self, objects, needs, time, and place.
- **A1:** routine transactions and short personal conversations.
- **A2:** connected accounts of plans, experiences, comparisons, and problems.
- **B1:** independent interaction, reasons, stories, polite negotiation, and familiar workplace tasks.

CEFR labels are planning guides, not certification claims. Each level should ultimately be validated through recorded learner performances against explicit can-do tasks.
