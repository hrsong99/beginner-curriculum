I can introduce myself and state what I am not  
1\. 私は \~です (watashi wa \~desu) (I am \[Name/Occupation/Nationality\])  
2\. 私は \~じゃありません (watashi wa \~ja arimasen) (I am not \[Occupation/Nationality\])

I can identify things and talk about who they belong to  
1\. これは \~です (kore wa \~desu) (This is \[Object\])  
2\. これは \~の \~です (kore wa \~no \~desu) (This is \[Someone's\] \[Object\])

I can talk about where places are and ask for directions  
1\. ここは \~です (koko wa \~desu) (Here is \[Place\])  
2\. \~は どこですか (\~wa doko desu ka) (Where is \[Place\]?)

I can ask for prices and request items at a store  
1\. \~は いくらですか (\~wa ikura desu ka) (How much is \[Item\]?)  
2\. \~を ください (\~o kudasai) (Please give me \[Item\])

I can state what or who is present in a location  
1\. \~に \[Object\] が あります (\~ni \[Object\] ga arimasu) (There is \[Object\] in/at \[Location\])  
2\. \~に \[Person/Animal\] が います (\~ni \[Person/Animal\] ga imasu) (There is \[Person/Animal\] in/at \[Location\])

I can state how many things there are and the quantity of actions  
1\. \~が \[Number/Counter\] あります/います (\~ga \[Number/Counter\] arimasu/imasu) (There are \[Number\] of \[Object/Person\])  
2\. たくさん / 少し \[Verb\]ます (takusan / sukoshi \[Verb\]masu) (I do \[Verb\] a lot / a little)

I can tell the time and describe start and end points  
1\. 今、\~時 \~分です (ima, \~ji \~fun desu) (Right now, it is \[Hour\] \[Minute\])  
2\. \~から \~までです (\~kara \~made desu) (It is from \[Time/Place\] to \[Time/Place\])

I can express where I am going and how I get there  
1\. \~へ 行きます (\~e ikimasu) (I go to \[Destination\])  
2\. \~で 行きます (\~de ikimasu) (I go by \[Means of Transportation\])

I can state the purpose of my visit and what I am going to do  
1\. \~へ \[Verb Stem\]に 行きます (\~e \[Verb Stem\]ni ikimasu) (I go to \[Location\] to \[Verb\])  
2\. \~へ \[Noun\]に 行きます (\~e \[Noun\]ni ikimasu) (I go to \[Location\] for \[Noun\])

I can describe my daily activities and where they happen  
1\. \~を \[Verb\]ます (\~o \[Verb\]masu) (I \[Verb\] \[Object\])  
2\. \~で \[Verb\]ます (\~de \[Verb\]masu) (I \[Verb\] at \[Location\])

I can invite someone to do something and suggest doing something together  
1\. \~ませんか (\~masen ka) (Won't you \[Verb\]?)  
2\. \~ましょう (\~mashou) (Let's \[Verb\])

I can describe the characteristics of things and places (Present)  
1\. \~は \[i-Adjective\]いです (\~wa \[i-Adjective\]i desu) (\[Subject\] is \[Adjective\])  
2\. \~は \[na-Adjective\]です (\~wa \[na-Adjective\] desu) (\[Subject\] is \[Adjective\])

I can talk about what I like and what I am good at  
1\. \~が 好きです (\~ga suki desu) (I like \[Noun\])  
2\. \~が 上手です (\~ga jouzu desu) (I am good at \[Noun\])

I can express what I want and what I want to do  
1\. \~が ほしいです (\~ga hoshii desu) (I want \[Noun\])  
2\. \~たいです (\~tai desu) (I want to \[Verb\])

I can talk about how things were in the past (Nouns & Adjectives)  
1\. \~でした (\~deshita) (It was \[Noun / na-Adjective\])  
2\. \~かったです (\~katta desu) (It was \[i-Adjective\])

I can talk about what I did and did not do in the past (Verbs)  
1\. \~ました (\~mashita) (I \[Verb\]ed)  
2\. \~ませんでした (\~masen deshita) (I did not \[Verb\])

I can ask for reasons and explain why something is the case  
1\. どうして \~ですか (doushite \~desu ka) (Why is it \[Situation\]?)  
2\. \~からです (\~kara desu) (It is because \[Reason\])

I can instruct someone to do something or ask for a favor  
1\. \~て ください (\~te kudasai) (Please \[Verb\])  
2\. \~ないで ください (\~naide kudasai) (Please do not \[Verb\])

I can describe what I am doing right now or a continuous state  
1\. \~て います (\~te imasu) (I am currently \[Verb\]ing)  
2\. まだ \~て いません (mada \~te imasen) (I have not \[Verb\]ed yet)

I can ask for permission and state what is not allowed  
1\. \~て もいいですか (\~te mo ii desu ka) (May I \[Verb\]?)  
2\. \~て はいけません (\~te wa ikemasen) (You must not \[Verb\])

I can connect actions and describe sequences  
1\. \~て、\~ます (\~te, \~masu) (I \[Verb\], and then \[Verb\])  
2\. \~てから、\~ます (\~te kara, \~masu) (After I \[Verb\], I \[Verb\])

I can connect descriptions to say multiple things about a subject  
1\. \~くて、\~です (\~kute, \~desu) (It is \[i-Adjective\] and \[Adjective/Noun\])  
2\. \~で、\~です (\~de, \~desu) (It is \[na-Adjective/Noun\] and \[Adjective/Noun\])

I can compare two items and state a preference  
1\. Aの ほうが Bより \~です (A no hou ga B yori \~desu) (A is more \[Adjective\] than B)  
2\. Aと Bと どちらが \~ですか (A to B to dochira ga \~desu ka) (Which is more \[Adjective\], A or B?)

I can change polite sentences into plain form and express my thoughts  
1\. \[Plain Form\] \+ と 思います (\[Plain Form\] \+ to omoimasu) (I think that \[Casual Sentence\])  
2\. \[Plain Form\] \+ でしょう (\[Plain Form\] \+ deshou) (It is probably \[Casual Sentence\])

I can quote what other people have said  
1\. \~と 言いました (\~to iimashita) (\[Person\] said that \[Casual Sentence\])  
2\. \~と 言って いました (\~to itte imashita) (\[Person\] was saying that \[Casual Sentence\])

I can talk about my abilities and hobbies as actions  
1\. \~ことが できます (\~koto ga dekimasu) (I can \[Verb\] / I am able to \[Verb\])  
2\. \~のが 好きです (\~no ga suki desu) (I like \[Verb\]ing)

I can talk about my past life experiences  
1\. \~た ことが あります (\~ta koto ga arimasu) (I have the experience of \[Verb\]ing)  
2\. \~た ことが ありません (\~ta koto ga arimasen) (I have never \[Verb\]ed)

I can give advice and make suggestions based on situations  
1\. \~た ほうが いいです (\~ta hou ga ii desu) (You should \[Verb\])  
2\. \~ない ほうが いいです (\~nai hou ga ii desu) (You had better not \[Verb\])

I can list various actions I do without a strict order  
1\. \~たり \~たり します (\~tari \~tari shimasu) (I do things like \[Verb\] and \[Verb\])  
2\. \~や \~など (\~ya \~nado) (Things like \[Noun\] and \[Noun\])

I can explain obligations and what is not necessary  
1\. \~なければ なりません (\~nakereba narimasen) (I must \[Verb\])  
2\. \~なくても いいです (\~nakutemo ii desu) (You do not have to \[Verb\])

I can describe when an action happens in relation to another  
1\. \~時、\~ (\~toki, \~) (When \[Situation\], \[Action\])  
2\. \~前に、\~ (\~mae ni, \~) (Before \[Action\], \[Action\])

I can talk about giving items and doing favors for others  
1\. \~に \~を あげます (\~ni \~o agemasu) (I give \[Item\] to \[Person\])  
2\. \~て あげます (\~te agemasu) (I do the favor of \[Verb\]ing for someone)

I can talk about receiving items and asking for favors from others  
1\. \~に \~を もらいます (\~ni \~o moraimasu) (I receive \[Item\] from \[Person\])  
2\. \~て もらいます (\~te moraimasu) (I have someone do the favor of \[Verb\]ing for me)

I can express gratitude when someone gives me something or does a favor  
1\. \~が \~を くれます (\~ga \~o kuremasu) (\[Someone\] gives \[Item\] to me)  
2\. \~て くれます (\~te kuremasu) (\[Someone\] does the favor of \[Verb\]ing for me)

I can describe a natural change in state  
1\. \~く なります (\~ku narimasu) (It becomes \[i-Adjective\])  
2\. \~に なります (\~ni narimasu) (It becomes \[na-Adjective/Noun\])

I can state my future intentions and definite plans  
1\. \~つもりです (\~tsumori desu) (I intend to \[Verb\])  
2\. \~予定です (\~yotei desu) (I am scheduled to \[Verb\] / My plan is \[Noun\])

I can explain a situation smoothly to give an excuse or introduce a request  
1\. \~んです (\~n desu) (It is the case that \[Explanation/Reason\])  
2\. \~んですが (\~n desu ga) (It is the case that \[Situation\], so...)

I can modify nouns with entire sentences to be more descriptive  
1\. \[Casual Sentence\] \+ 名詞 (\[Casual Sentence\] \+ \[Noun\]) (The \[Noun\] that \[Action/State\])  
2\. \~という \+ 名詞 (\~to iu \+ \[Noun\]) (The \[Noun\] called/named "\~")

I can express doing two things at once or doing something excessively  
1\. \~ながら (\~nagara) (While \[Verb\]ing)  
2\. \~すぎます (\~sugimasu) (To \[Verb\] or be \[Adjective\] too much)

I can guess how something is based on appearance or report what I heard  
1\. \[Stem\] \+ そうです (\[Stem\] \+ sou desu) (It looks like / seems like \[Adjective/Verb\])  
2\. \[Casual Sentence\] \+ そうです (\[Casual Sentence\] \+ sou desu) (I heard that \[Sentence\])

I can express the purpose of an action  
1\. \~ために (\~tame ni) (In order to \[Verb\] / For the sake of \[Noun\])  
2\. \~ように (\~you ni) (So that \[Condition/Potential Verb\] becomes true)

I can describe how easy or difficult an action is to perform  
1\. \~やすいです (\~yasui desu) (It is easy to \[Verb\])  
2\. \~にくいです (\~nikui desu) (It is difficult to \[Verb\])

I can express conditions and natural results (If/When)  
1\. \~たら (\~tara) (If / When \[Action/State\] happens)  
2\. \~と (\~to) (Whenever / If \[Action\] happens, \[Natural Result\] happens)

I can talk about actions done completely or actions done by mistake  
1\. \~て しまいます (\~te shimaimasu) (I will finish \[Verb\]ing / I will accidentally \[Verb\])  
2\. \~て しまいました (\~te shimaimashita) (I completely \[Verb\]ed / I unfortunately \[Verb\]ed)

I can describe the exact timing of an action  
1\. \~る ところです (\~ru tokoro desu) (I am just about to \[Verb\])  
2\. \~た ばかりです (\~ta bakari desu) (I have just finished \[Verb\]ing)

I can describe doing something in advance or trying something out  
1\. \~て おきます (\~te okimasu) (I will \[Verb\] in advance / for later)  
2\. \~て みます (\~te mimasu) (I will try \[Verb\]ing to see how it is)

I can describe the start and end of an ongoing action  
1\. \~はじめます (\~hajimemasu) (I start to \[Verb\])  
2\. \~おわります (\~owarimasu) (I finish \[Verb\]ing)

I can express limits or state that nothing else is available  
1\. \~だけです (\~dake desu) (There is only \[Noun/Action\])  
2\. \~しか \[Negative Verb\] (\~shika \[Negative Verb\]) (There is nothing but / only \[Noun\])
