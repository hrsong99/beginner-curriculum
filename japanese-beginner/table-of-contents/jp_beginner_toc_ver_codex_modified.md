# Japanese Beginner Curriculum: Pattern Stack

This version is designed for a speaking-first beginner curriculum built around short, high-payoff lessons for a `15-25 minute` class.

- Each lesson should introduce only one real grammar load: one conjugation family, one contrast, or one dense vocab system.
- If a lesson uses a dense vocab system like position words, dates, or counters, the grammar frame should stay fixed.
- Two patterns are only paired when they are structurally close enough to practice together without overload.
- The core goal is not "cover all beginner grammar fast." The goal is "help absolute beginners start talking right away and keep building without confusion."

---

## Module 1: Immediate Survival Japanese (Lessons 1-5)

Speaking payoff: learners can introduce themselves, identify objects, ask where things are, and talk about basic location.

Lesson 1:
I can introduce myself and ask simple yes/no identity questions
1. 私は ~です (watashi wa ~ desu) (I am ~)
2. ~ですか / はい、そうです / いいえ、ちがいます (~ desu ka / hai, sou desu / iie, chigaimasu) (Are you ~? / Yes, that's right / No, that's not right)

Lesson 2:
I can identify things and say who they belong to
1. これは ~です (kore wa ~ desu) (This is ~)
2. これは ~の~です (kore wa ~ no ~ desu) (This is ~'s ~)

Lesson 3:
I can ask where something is and answer simply
1. ~は どこですか (~ wa doko desu ka) (Where is ~?)
2. ~は ここ/そこ/あそこです (~ wa koko/soko/asoko desu) (~ is here/there/over there)

Lesson 4:
I can say where something is more precisely
1. Xは Yの 上/下/前/後ろです (X wa Y no ue/shita/mae/ushiro desu) (X is above/below/in front of/behind Y)
2. Xは Yの 中/右/左です (X wa Y no naka/migi/hidari desu) (X is in/to the right/to the left of Y)

Lesson 5:
I can say what or who is in a place
1. ~に [Thing] が あります (~ ni [thing] ga arimasu) (There is [thing] in/at ~)
2. ~に [Person/Animal] が います (~ ni [person/animal] ga imasu) (There is [person/animal] in/at ~)

---

## Module 2: Daily Life Basics (Lessons 6-10)

Speaking payoff: learners can talk about basic actions, routines, times, dates, and what to do when they do not understand.

Lesson 6:
I can say what I do and ask if someone does it
1. [Noun]を [Verb]ます ([noun] o [verb] masu) (I [verb] [noun])
2. [Noun]を [Verb]ますか ([noun] o [verb] masu ka) (Do you [verb] [noun]?)

Lesson 7:
I can say when something happens and the time range
1. ~時に [Verb]ます (~ ji ni [verb] masu) (I [verb] at ~ o'clock)
2. ~時から ~時までです (~ ji kara ~ ji made desu) (It is from ~ o'clock to ~ o'clock)

Lesson 8:
I can say days and dates
1. 今日は ~曜日です (kyou wa ~ youbi desu) (Today is ~)
2. 今日は ~月 ~日です (kyou wa ~ gatsu ~ nichi desu) (Today is ~ / the date is ~)

Lesson 9:
I can talk about routine frequency
1. いつも/よく ~ます (itsumo/yoku ~ masu) (I always / often ~)
2. あまり ~ません (amari ~ masen) (I do not ~ very much / very often)

Lesson 10:
I can manage the conversation when I do not understand
1. もういちど おねがいします (mou ichido onegaishimasu) (One more time, please)
2. わかりません / ゆっくり おねがいします (wakarimasen / yukkuri onegaishimasu) (I don't understand / Please speak slowly)

---

## Module 3: Practical Out-and-About Japanese (Lessons 11-15)

Speaking payoff: learners can shop, move around, make simple plans, and count basic things and people.

Lesson 11:
I can shop for basic things and ask the price
1. ~は いくらですか (~ wa ikura desu ka) (How much is ~?)
2. ~を ください (~ o kudasai) (Please give me ~)

Lesson 12:
I can say where I am going and how I get there
1. ~に/へ 行きます (~ ni/e ikimasu) (I go to ~)
2. ~で 行きます (~ de ikimasu) (I go by ~)

Lesson 13:
I can say why I am going somewhere
1. ~へ [Verb Stem]に 行きます (~ e [verb stem] ni ikimasu) (I go to ~ to [verb])
2. ~へ [Noun]に 行きます (~ e [noun] ni ikimasu) (I go to ~ for [noun])

Lesson 14:
I can invite someone and suggest doing something together
1. ~ませんか (~ masen ka) (Won't you ~?)
2. ~ましょう (~ mashou) (Let's ~)

Lesson 15:
I can say how many things or people there are
1. [Thing]が [Number + つ] あります ([thing] ga [number + tsu] arimasu) (There are [number] [things])
2. [Person]が [Number + 人] います ([person] ga [number + nin] imasu) (There are [number] people)

---

## Module 4: Description, Preferences, and Wants (Lessons 16-20)

Speaking payoff: learners can describe things and people, talk about likes and dislikes, and say what they want.

Lesson 16:
I can describe things with i-adjectives
1. ~は [i-Adjective]いです (~ wa [i-adjective] desu) (~ is [i-adjective])
2. ~は [i-Adjective]くないです (~ wa [i-adjective] ku nai desu) (~ is not [i-adjective])

Lesson 17:
I can describe things with na-adjectives
1. ~は [na-Adjective]です (~ wa [na-adjective] desu) (~ is [na-adjective])
2. ~は [na-Adjective]じゃありません (~ wa [na-adjective] ja arimasen) (~ is not [na-adjective])

Lesson 18:
I can talk about what I like and do not like
1. ~が 好きです (~ ga suki desu) (I like ~)
2. ~が きらいです (~ ga kirai desu) (I dislike ~)

Lesson 19:
I can say what I want and what I want to do
1. ~が ほしいです (~ ga hoshii desu) (I want ~)
2. ~たいです (~ tai desu) (I want to ~)

Lesson 20:
I can compare two things and ask which one is better
1. Aより Bのほうが ~です (A yori B no hou ga ~ desu) (B is more ~ than A)
2. Aと Bと どちらが ~ですか (A to B to dochira ga ~ desu ka) (Which is more ~, A or B?)

---

## Module 5: Talking About Past Actions and Basic Interaction (Lessons 21-25)

Speaking payoff: learners can talk about the past, give reasons, connect actions, and handle simple polite interaction.

Lesson 21:
I can talk about what I did and did not do
1. ~ました (~ mashita) (I did ~)
2. ~ませんでした (~ masen deshita) (I did not do ~)

Lesson 22:
I can talk about how things were in the past
1. ~でした (~ deshita) (~ was [noun / na-adjective])
2. ~かったです (~ katta desu) (~ was [i-adjective])

Lesson 23:
I can ask for reasons and give simple explanations
1. どうして ~ですか (doushite ~ desu ka) (Why is / was ~?)
2. ~からです (~ kara desu) (It is because ~)

Lesson 24:
I can connect actions into a natural sequence
1. ~て、~ます (~ te, ~ masu) (~ and then ~)
2. ~てから、~ます (~ te kara, ~ masu) (After ~, ~)

Lesson 25:
I can make polite requests and ask for permission
1. ~て ください (~ te kudasai) (Please ~)
2. ~て もいいですか (~ te mo ii desu ka) (May I ~?)

---

## Module 6: Ongoing Actions and More Natural Linking (Lessons 26-30)

Speaking payoff: learners can talk about what is happening now, state rules, link descriptions, and start switching into casual speech.

Lesson 26:
I can describe what is happening now and what has not happened yet
1. ~て います (~ te imasu) (I am ~ing / it is in a ~ state)
2. まだ ~て いません (mada ~ te imasen) (I have not ~ yet)

Lesson 27:
I can talk about rules and what is not allowed
1. ~て はいけません (~ te wa ikemasen) (You must not ~)
2. ここで ~て はいけません (koko de ~ te wa ikemasen) (You must not ~ here)

Lesson 28:
I can connect descriptions and say more than one thing smoothly
1. ~くて、~です (~ kute, ~ desu) (~ is [i-adjective] and ~)
2. ~で、~です (~ de, ~ desu) (~ is [na-adjective/noun] and ~)

Lesson 29:
I can start using casual present and negative forms
1. [Dictionary Form] ([dictionary form]) (casual present / future)
2. [ない Form] ([nai form]) (casual negative)

Lesson 30:
I can talk casually about the past
1. [た Form] ([ta form]) (casual past)
2. [なかった Form] ([nakatta form]) (casual negative past)

---

## Module 7: Expanding What I Can Say (Lessons 31-35)

Speaking payoff: learners can share opinions, report speech, talk about ability, talk about experiences, and list examples more naturally.

Lesson 31:
I can say what I think and what someone said
1. [Plain Form] + と 思います ([plain form] + to omoimasu) (I think that ~)
2. [Plain Form] + と 言いました ([plain form] + to iimashita) (Someone said that ~)

Lesson 32:
I can talk about my abilities and action-based hobbies
1. ~ことが できます (~ koto ga dekimasu) (I can ~ / I am able to ~)
2. ~のが 好きです (~ no ga suki desu) (I like ~ing)

Lesson 33:
I can talk about my past experiences and give simple advice
1. ~た ことが あります (~ ta koto ga arimasu) (I have done ~ before)
2. ~た ほうが いいです (~ ta hou ga ii desu) (You should ~)

Lesson 34:
I can list examples of things and actions
1. ~や ~など (~ ya ~ nado) (~ and ~, for example)
2. ~たり ~たり します (~ tari ~ tari shimasu) (I do things like ~ and ~)

Lesson 35:
I can say what I must do and what I do not have to do
1. ~なければ なりません (~ nakereba narimasen) (I must ~)
2. ~なくても いいです (~ nakutemo ii desu) (I do not have to ~)

---

## Module 8: Real Interaction and Everyday Nuance (Lessons 36-40)

Speaking payoff: learners can talk about timing, giving and receiving, favors, changes, and future plans in everyday conversation.

Lesson 36:
I can talk about what happens before and after something else
1. [Dictionary Form / Nounの] + 前に、~ます ([dictionary form / noun no] + mae ni, ~ masu) (Before ~, ~)
2. [た Form / Nounの] + あとで、~ます ([ta form / noun no] + ato de, ~ masu) (After ~, ~)

Lesson 37:
I can talk about giving and receiving things
1. ~に ~を あげます (~ ni ~ o agemasu) (I give ~ to ~)
2. ~に ~を もらいます (~ ni ~ o moraimasu) (I receive ~ from ~)

Lesson 38:
I can talk about favors and help between people
1. ~て くれます (~ te kuremasu) (Someone does ~ for me)
2. ~て もらいます (~ te moraimasu) (I have someone do ~ for me)

Lesson 39:
I can describe changes in state
1. ~く なります (~ ku narimasu) (It becomes ~ [i-adjective])
2. ~に なります (~ ni narimasu) (It becomes ~ [na-adjective / noun])

Lesson 40:
I can talk about plans and intentions
1. ~つもりです (~ tsumori desu) (I intend to ~)
2. ~予定です (~ yotei desu) (I am scheduled to ~ / It is planned)

## Optional Upper-Beginner Bridge (Lessons 41-45)

Use this block if you want to extend the curriculum without overloading the 40-lesson beginner core.

Lesson 41:
I can explain a situation more softly and set up a request
1. ~んです (~ n desu) (The thing is ~ / It is that ~)
2. ~んですが (~ n desu ga) (The thing is ~, and...)

Lesson 42:
I can describe nouns with fuller information
1. [Plain Form] + 名詞 ([plain form] + noun) (the noun that ~)
2. ~という + 名詞 (~ to iu + noun) (the noun called ~)

Lesson 43:
I can say I will try something and do something in preparation
1. ~て みます (~ te mimasu) (I will try ~)
2. ~て おきます (~ te okimasu) (I will do ~ in advance)

Lesson 44:
I can say whether an action is easy or difficult
1. ~やすいです (~ yasui desu) (It is easy to ~)
2. ~にくいです (~ nikui desu) (It is difficult to ~)

Lesson 45:
I can talk about conditions and natural results
1. ~たら (~ tara) (If / when ~)
2. ~と (~ to) (Whenever / when ~, natural result)

## Built-In Usage Notes

These should be embedded into the relevant lessons rather than treated as major standalone grammar lessons.

- Family and politeness: introduce `母 / お母さん`, `父 / お父さん`, and the inside/outside distinction when learners begin talking about people close to them.
- Counters beyond `~つ` and `~人`: add high-frequency counters like `~枚`, `~本`, and `~杯` as support vocabulary in shopping, food, and daily-life lessons.
- Question words: recycle `何`, `だれ`, `どこ`, `いつ`, and `どれ` repeatedly across early lessons instead of isolating them.
- Position words: keep the active set small in class. Do not try to force every location word into one short lesson.
- Conversation repair: keep using `もういちど おねがいします`, `ゆっくり おねがいします`, and `わかりません` from Lesson 10 onward in every class.
