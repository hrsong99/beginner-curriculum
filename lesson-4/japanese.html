REVIEW: LESSONS 1, 2 & 3

"Let's review — translate each sentence into Japanese."
「ふくしゅうしましょう。にほんごにしてみてね。」
LESSON 1: です (I am / he is)
PATTERN: [person]はwa [noun / adjective]ですdesu。
I am a student.
He is kind.
She is from Tokyo.
WORDS Q1: わたしwatashi(I)   がくせいgakusei(student)
Q2: かれkare(he)   しんせつshinsetsu(kind)
Q3: かのじょkanojo(she)   とうきょうTōkyō(Tokyo)
LESSON 2: これ・それ・あれ (this / that / over there)
PATTERN: これkore / それsore / あれareはwa [noun]ですdesu。
This is a book.
That is my pen.
That (over there) is the teacher's bag.
WORDS Q1: これkore(this)   ほんhon(book)
Q2: それsore(that)   わたしwatashi(I)   ペンpen(pen)
Q3: あれare(that over there)   せんせいsensei(teacher)   かばんkaban(bag)
LESSON 3: どこ (where)
PATTERN: [noun]はwaどこdokoですdesuかka？
[noun]はwaここkoko / そこsoko / あそこasokoですdesu。
Where is the book?
The pen is there.
Where is my bag?
WORDS Q1: ほんhon(book)   どこdoko(where)
Q2: ペンpen(pen)   そこsoko(there)
Q3: わたしwatashi(I)   かばんkaban(bag)   どこdoko(where)

TODAY'S PATTERNS

1. There IS a [thing] — ありますarimasu

• へやheyaにniテレビterebiがgaありますarimasu。
There is a TV in the room.
• いえieにniほんhonがgaありますarimasu。
There is a book at home.

2. There IS a [person / animal] — いますimasu

• きょうしつkyōshitsuにniせんせいsenseiがgaいますimasu。
There is a teacher in the classroom.
• へやheyaにniねこnekoがgaいますimasu。
There is a cat in the room.
REMEMBER
ありますarimasu → for THINGS (book, pen, desk...)
いますimasu → for LIVING things (people, animals)
